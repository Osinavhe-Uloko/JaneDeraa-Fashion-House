00  Brand Foundations
from the Jane Deraa guidelines
Ready to wear · Custom · Bespoke
Timeless elegance in every stitch. Classic lines, modern sensibility.
Logo — main lockup
Vertical lockup
Sub-mark
The mark and wordmark are never separated, stretched or compressed. The horizontal and vertical lockups carry official material; the sub-mark is for display use — avatars, wallpapers, watermarks and merchandise.
Palette
Green#1D4429
Gold#AF8A2F
Black#000000
Grey#B7B7B7
White#FFFFFF
Green carries the brand blocks. Gold is a stroke and a rule, never a fill. Screens stay predominantly white, grey and black.
Primary — Montserrat
About us
Navigation, buttons, labels and body copy. Light and regular for running text, semibold and bold for headings. Uppercase labels track at 0.14em.
Secondary — Clash Display
Timeless elegance
Editorial display only — hero lines, page titles, collection names, product names. Never set below 16px.

Bespoke
One garment,
one wearer
Philosophy
A bespoke garment starts with nothing — no pattern, no size, no assumption about the body that will wear it. We take some thirty measurements, cut a paper pattern that exists only for you, and build the garment across three fittings. It takes twelve weeks. It is meant to be worn for twenty years.
